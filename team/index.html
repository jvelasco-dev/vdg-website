Meet the Doctors
Dr. Lida Vargas, DDS

With more than 30 years of experience in dentistry, Dr. Vargas combines extensive clinical expertise with advanced technology to provide exceptional, personalized care. After earning her dental degree in Colombia in 1994, she received her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from the University of Maryland in 2008. Since then, she has pursued advanced training in implant dentistry, orthodontics, laser dentistry, sleep medicine, TMJ therapy, myofunctional therapy, and cosmetic and facial aesthetics. Dr. Vargas is committed to staying at the forefront of modern dentistry, incorporating technologies such as CAD/CAM, CBCT imaging, laser dentistry, and PRF treatments to enhance patient comfort and treatment outcomes. Her dedication to excellence has earned her recognition as a Northern Virginia Magazine Top Doctor in 2017, 2021, 2025, and 2026, as well as ICOI Fellowship and AGD Fellowship honors. At Virginia Dental Group, Dr. Vargas combines experience, innovation, and compassionate care to help every patient achieve a healthy, confident smile.

Dr. Santiago Rueda, DDS

An accomplished dentist dedicated to combining advanced technology, clinical expertise, and personalized care. He earned his DDS from the University of Maryland Dental School in 2005, followed by a General Practice Residency at Lehigh Valley Hospital in Pennsylvania. Dr. Rueda is Invisalign-certified and has advanced training in guided implant surgery, bone regeneration, minimally invasive periodontal procedures, and Versah® osseodensification. He also utilizes Fotona® laser technology for restorative and periodontal treatments, DEKA Quietnight for snoring, and PRF techniques to enhance surgical and aesthetic outcomes. In addition, Dr. Rueda is certified in Botox® and dermal fillers through the American Academy of Facial Esthetics. He is an active member of the American Dental Association, Virginia Dental Association, and Global Dental Implant Academy. With a passion for innovation and aesthetics, Dr. Rueda provides comprehensive dental care focused on improving oral health, function, and confidence.
